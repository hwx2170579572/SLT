## Closest evidence for the candidate boundary

The candidate idea under review is a scene-level correlated mode, with each observed actor assigned legal movement/path progress and mode-conditioned entry/clearance events for named conflict resources, retained until the SAC critic consumes `z_t,a_t`. These papers show why the broad components are not novel by themselves:

- **GraphAD** already keeps actor modalities as separate nodes, measures agent–agent edges at synchronized future times, and connects future trajectories to static map geometry. Its result is open-loop: average nuScenes L2 0.68 m and collision 0.12%. The defensible difference cannot be “mode-specific dynamic graph”; it must specify path-ordered resource events and an online critic experiment.
- **BeTop** turns future multi-agent trajectory braids into interaction-topology labels and uses those labels for local attention. It reports reactive Test14-Inter PDMScore 0.894 versus PlanTF 0.871, but does not validate off-policy SAC state encoding. It blocks generic future-topology and yielding-relation claims.
- **SWIFT** has a small-world directed vehicle interaction graph, flow-regime-conditioned connectivity, and direct/co-influenced/co-influencing relation channels. It is a close structural interaction-graph prior, but is supervised forecasting. The arXiv v1 is in-window and arXiv comments confirm TPAMI acceptance; the formal issue/publication date remains unverified.
- **NEST** uses density-conditioned small-world hyperedges for group interactions. It blocks generic hypergraph or density-adaptive relation novelty; reported 12-agent timing comparisons use different GPU models across papers.
- **CfDCA** explicitly has conflict-zone resource nodes, vehicle requirement/acquisition edges, entry/exit/clearance, and priority ordering. This blocks resource/event primitives as novelty alone, but the paper’s deterministic fixed-path protocol is not a learned value function.
- **ModeSeq** models dependence and confidence among each actor’s multiple forecast modes. It does not give a calibrated joint distribution over the entire scene, so a scene-level joint-mode claim remains a precise hypothesis to test, not a settled gap.

T²SG and SeqGrowGraph further establish lane topology generation; RAP and MomAD establish role-aware joint prediction/planning and historical-query conditioning. The project’s exact SUMO map means their map-perception scores do not establish an RL return improvement. None of these sources is a fair quantitative baseline for a critic-only SAC redesign.

No new novelty claim is supported yet. A possible narrow contribution must distinguish (i) a correlated *scene-level* mode distribution from per-actor modalities and braid edges, (ii) ordered, named resource-zone occupancy intervals from distance-based graph adjacency or a fixed protocol, and (iii) a feature that improves SAC action ranking from an auxiliary forecaster/planner. The existing observation contract and background-vehicle route limits are in [environment_evidence.md](environment_evidence.md); no evaluation-time future labels or hidden future routes may enter the critic input.
